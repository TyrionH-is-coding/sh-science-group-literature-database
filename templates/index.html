How to use

Usage guide

This page documents the current workflow. Add new notes here whenever the site gains a feature.

  1. 1. Sign in and enter APS Review

    Enter your user name on the login screen, then choose APS Review. The name is used for uploads and saved LLM draft history.

  2. 2. Browse the literature library

    Use search, priority, module filters, and pagination to narrow the paper table. Each page shows 50 papers by default.

  3. 3. Open and select from a paper

    Open a paper from the detail panel. In the rendered article page, click a sentence or highlight text manually to add it to the Evidence Library.

    The small left-side Evidence Library panel on the article page shows selected sentences and lets you remove them immediately.

  4. 4. Ask PaperQA

    Ask a focused APS question. When PaperQA is configured on the server, cited sentences can be checked and added to the Evidence Library.

  5. 5. Compose an article draft

    Click Compose from the Evidence Library, arrange selected sentences into paragraph plans, set approximate length, then generate a citation-key based Nature-style synthesis draft.

  6. 6. Check draft history

    Generated paragraphs and article drafts are saved under the current user account after successful LLM generation.

  7. 7. Upload files

    Use the upload panel for manual PDFs, Markdown abstracts, or plain-text notes. A PMID is required, and the current user name is recorded with the upload.
