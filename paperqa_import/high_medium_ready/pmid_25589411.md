# Supplementary Material to Müller-Calleja et al. “Cofactor Independent Antiphospholipid Antibodies Activate the NLRP3-inflammasome via Endosomal NADPH-oxidase. Implications for the Antiphospholipid Syndrome” (Thromb Haemost 2015; 113.4)

![](images/b19e21085b0d9e6f5b5ab3ceac8df8db6be934ab06bcf2fe5429fd3eb0d5e6ec.jpg)  
Suppl. Figure 1: aPL mediated IL-1 $\beta$ induction in C57BL/6J could be blocked by NFA and extracellular RNase. Murine monocytes isolated from the spleens of C57BL/6 mice were stimulated for up to 8 hours with HL5B (100 ng/ml) either alone or together with NFA (0.1 mM) or RNase (10 $\mu$ g/ml). Relative expression of IL-1 $\beta$ mRNA was normalized to control IgG stimulation and $\beta$ -actin expression. Values represent mean +/- SD of 3 independent experiments.

Suppl. Figure 2: R848 mediated IL-1 $\beta$ induction. Murine monocytes isolated from the spleens of C57BL/6 mice were stimulated for up to 8 hours with R848 (1 $\mu$ g/ml) (A) Relative expression of IL-1 $\beta$ mRNA was normalized to $\beta$ -actin expression. (B) IL-1 $\beta$ cytokine secretion was measured by ELISA. Values represent mean +/- SD of 3 independent experiments.

Suppl. Figure 3: Inflammasome activation in genetically modified mouse strains. Murine monocytes isolated from the spleens of C57BL/6, p74phox $^{-/-}$ , TLR2/4 DKO and TLR9 $^{-/-}$ mice were stimulated for 2 hours with HL5B, control IgG (100 ng/ml each) or R848 (1 μg/ml) (A) Caspase-1 activity normalized to unstimulated cells of the respective mice strains. (B) Relative expression of NLRP3 mRNA normalized to β-actin expression. Values represent mean +/- SD of 3 independent experiments.

Suppl. Figure 4: aPL mediated IL-1 $\beta$ induction in NLRP3 $^{-/-}$ mice could be blocked by IRS957 and RNase. Murine monocytes isolated from the spleens of C57BL/6 mice were stimulated for up to 8 hours with HL5B (100 ng/ml) either alone or together with IRS957 (0.5 $\mu$ M) or RNase (10 $\mu$ g/ml). Relative expression of IL-1 $\beta$ mRNA was normalized to control IgG stimulation and $\beta$ -actin expression. Values represent mean +/- SD of 4 independent experiments.
